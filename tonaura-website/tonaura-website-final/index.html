The honest version

Tradition and evidence, kept separate

Solfeggio frequencies come from tradition, not clinical trials. We're not going to pretend otherwise — but we didn't stop at tradition either.

Rooted in tradition

  • Nine tones, each carrying a centuries-old association — fear, change, transformation, connection.
  • No peer-reviewed claims attached to the numerology itself.
  • Here for how it feels, not as a diagnosis or a cure.

Backed by research

  • Binaural beats — a real, measurable brainwave entrainment effect.
  • Isochronic pulses — the same principle, works without headphones.
  • Layered underneath the tones, never presented as the tones themselves.
